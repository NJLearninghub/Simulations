Dwarka 1-Day Itinerary

Seniors & Toddler Optimized (86.6 km)

START

Dwarka Railway Station

Arrival Point

Pre-booked SUV pickup. Arrange porters for luggage.

1

Dwarkadhish Temple

Morning Visit • 3.5 km away

Early morning darshan to avoid heat. Use accessible lines.

2

Gomti Ghat

Scenic Relaxation • 1.0 km away

View from the top to avoid 56 steps. Good spot for baby rest.

3

Rukmini Devi Mandir

Quick Stop • 2.5 km away

Short walk from parking. Pre-lunch detour.

4

Nageshwar Jyotirlinga

Afternoon • 16.0 km away

Flatter grounds, massive Shiva statue. Good restroom facilities.

5

Sudarshan Setu

Scenic Drive • 23.0 km away

Drive across the new bridge to avoid the bumpy ferry ride.

6

Bet Dwarka

Island Visit • 2.0 km away

Historic Krishna residence. Take E-rickshaw from bridge drop-off.

7

Bhadkeshwar Mahadev

Sunset • 35.0 km away (Return to mainland)

Smooth pathway out to the sea. Perfect sunset for the family.

END

Dwarka Bus Station

Departure • 2.5 km away

Board sleeper bus for comfortable return journey.
